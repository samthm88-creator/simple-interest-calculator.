# simple-interest-calculator.

Simple Interest Calculator

Introduction

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple tool designed to calculate the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specific period of time at a fixed interest rate.

Formula

Simple Interest is calculated using the following formula:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal amount (initial amount of money)
- R = Annual interest rate (percentage)
- T = Time period (in years)
- SI = Simple Interest

Example

If the principal amount is $1,000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

SI = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = $100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Total Amount = $1,100

Features

- Calculates simple interest accurately.
- Accepts principal amount, interest rate, and time period.
- Displays the calculated interest.
- Calculates the total amount payable.

Purpose

This calculator helps users quickly calculate simple interest and understand how interest accumulates over a given period.
